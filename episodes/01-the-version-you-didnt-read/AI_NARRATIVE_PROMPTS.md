# AI Narrative Shot Prompt Pack — S01E01

These prompts are for narrative plates only. Product footage must be authentic DocMate capture.

## Base continuity prompt

Use the same characters in every shot:

- Maya: naturally beautiful East Asian woman, early 30s, long dark hair, cream knit top, relaxed neutral trousers, warm expressive eyes.
- Daniel: handsome East Asian man, early 30s, neat dark hair, dark casual overshirt, understated appearance.
- Milo: exceptionally adorable small-to-medium fluffy dog, cream-and-caramel coat, very bright soulful eyes, soft expressive face.
- Same cozy modern apartment at night, half-packed moving boxes, warm practical lamps, measuring tape, folded blanket.
- Naturalistic short-film photography, believable skin, subtle film grain, restrained color, static or slow handheld camera.
- No glossy commercial lighting, no posing, no logos, no readable phone UI, no fake app screen.

## Still 01 — family anchor
Maya measuring the wall, Daniel folding a box, Milo foreground with chin resting on cardboard box edge. Milo must be immediately lovable.

## Still 02 — phone message
Daniel over-shoulder reading phone. Screen unreadable/neutral for later overlay. Maya packs in background. Milo visible if composition allows.

## Still 03 — hesitation
Close detail of Daniel's hand and generic phone document, thumb stopping just before a final action. Maya soft background.

## Still 04 — Milo reveal
Close-up of Milo beside boxes, warm light, natural sleepy eyes opening. Emotional but not manipulative.

## Still 05 — couple reaction
Maya and Daniel shoulder-to-shoulder reading phone. Quiet concern, no shock acting.

## Still 06 — aftermath
Maya on floor beside Milo, Daniel lowering phone, boxes around them. Thoughtful pause.

## Negative direction
Avoid:
- exaggerated crying
- angry landlord imagery
- comedy pet tricks
- influencer styling
- luxury apartment
- hyper-perfect plastic skin
- neon tech lighting
- fake app UI
- fake readable text
- floating phones
- SaaS ad graphics
