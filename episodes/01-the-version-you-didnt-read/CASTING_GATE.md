# Casting Gate — S01E01

## Rejected reference
The first family reference is rejected.

Reason:
- male lead not attractive enough for the intended cinematic pull
- female lead not attractive enough for the intended cinematic pull
- Milo not cute/emotionally magnetic enough

Do not animate or use that reference in production.

## New casting bar

### Maya
Must read immediately as a beautiful leading actress in a grounded contemporary drama:
- exceptional facial harmony
- luminous expressive eyes
- elegant, warm, intelligent presence
- natural beauty, not influencer styling
- believable as a real partner in a lived-in apartment

### Daniel
Must read immediately as a handsome leading actor:
- strong but gentle facial structure
- expressive eyes
- understated masculine charm
- polished enough to hold attention, but not fashion-ad posing

### Milo
Must be the most emotionally magnetic element in the frame:
- extremely cute teddy-bear face
- large soulful eyes
- tiny black nose
- plush cream/caramel fur
- realistic anatomy
- gentle trusting expression
- viewers should immediately understand why losing the dog would be unacceptable

## Lock rule
Do not proceed to animation until:
1. both human leads clearly meet the attractiveness bar;
2. their chemistry looks natural;
3. Milo is exceptionally cute;
4. all three can be kept visually consistent across shots.
