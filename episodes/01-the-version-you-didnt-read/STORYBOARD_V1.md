# Editing rhythm

The film must feel slower than a normal app ad.

Recommended average shot length before product reveal: 5–7 seconds.

The pet-clause Compare finding is the visual climax, not the logo.

Do not cut away from the finding too quickly.

# Non-negotiables

- Same Maya / Daniel / Milo across AI shots.
- Milo is visibly lovable before the reveal.
- No generated fake DocMate interface.
- Product footage must use the current synthetic fixture.
- No claim of legal interpretation.
- No villain framing of landlord.
- No happy "problem solved" ending.
- Episode ends on a better decision: **pause, review, clarify.**
