# Character & Visual Bible — S01E01

## Continuity priority

The audience must recognize the same couple and the same dog across every narrative shot.

The dog is the emotional anchor of the episode.

## Maya

- East Asian woman, early 30s.
- Naturally beautiful rather than fashion-editorial.
- Warm, expressive eyes; elegant but lived-in appearance.
- Long dark hair, simple natural styling.
- Wardrobe: cream knit top, relaxed neutral trousers, minimal jewelry.
- Personality on screen: practical, affectionate, a little tired from moving, never melodramatic.
- Performance note: small facial reactions are better than obvious surprise.

## Daniel

- East Asian man, early 30s.
- Handsome in a natural, understated way.
- Calm face, neat dark hair, casual dark shirt or overshirt.
- Looks credible as someone who notices details but is not obsessively suspicious.
- Performance note: hesitation should be subtle — a thumb stops, eyes refocus, then he checks.

## Milo

- Small-to-medium fluffy dog.
- Cream-and-caramel coat.
- Extremely cute, bright soulful eyes, soft expressive face.
- Must feel like a real loved family dog, not a commercial mascot.
- The audience should emotionally register Milo before the lease clause is revealed.
- Avoid novelty styling, costumes, bows, exaggerated cartoon proportions.

### Milo's emotional beats

1. **Opening:** resting beside a half-packed box, head on the edge.
2. **Domestic future:** Maya casually references where Milo's bed will go.
3. **Reveal:** cut to Milo asleep or looking up — no comedy.
4. **Ending:** Milo walks between boxes or settles beside the couple.

The story works only if the viewer has already bonded with him.

## Apartment

- Cozy modern apartment at night.
- Warm practical lamps, not glossy commercial lighting.
- Half-packed boxes, packing tape, measuring tape, folded blanket.
- Lived-in but tidy.
- No luxury penthouse aesthetic.
- No conspicuous brand logos.

## Camera language

- Natural 35mm-equivalent feeling for most human scenes.
- 50–70mm feeling for quiet reaction details.
- Static or slow handheld.
- Moderate shallow depth of field.
- Believable skin texture.
- Subtle film grain.
- Warm highlights / neutral shadows.
- No hyper-saturated social-ad look.

## Product transition

The cinematic world should not morph into a fake generated app.

When DocMate appears, cut cleanly to a real screen capture.

That contrast is intentional:

**fictional story context → authentic product evidence.**
