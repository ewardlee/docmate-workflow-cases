# DocMate Story Bible

## Core thesis

People do not want "PDF features." They want to avoid a mistake, finish a task, remember what happened, or find an important document later.

Every DocMate story therefore follows:

> **Problem appears → document enters DocMate → context is understood → action is taken → a durable record remains → the next action is obvious**

The recurring dramatic question is:

> **What could go wrong if I just tap Sign / Send / Pay / Ignore?**

## Creative grammar

### 1. Start with a human moment, not the app

Bad opening:
> "DocMate is a powerful AI PDF productivity suite."

Good opening:
> "Nothing major changed. Just sign the renewal when you can."

### 2. Show one hidden risk

Do not stack ten features. One meaningful change is enough.

Examples:
- notice period: 30 → 90 days
- salary: $92,000 → $88,000
- payment terms: Net 30 → Net 15
- renewal date moved forward
- account number changed
- signature page added
- warranty expires next month

### 3. The product reveal is evidence

The cinematic scene stops.
The real DocMate UI appears.
The user sees the exact page, change, amount, date, or version.

### 4. The ending is a completed job

Avoid ending on "feature used."

End on:
- reviewed version saved
- correct version signed
- reminder set
- sensitive information redacted
- fingerprint checked
- history preserved
- document found again

# Story Series A — One Line Changed

## 01. "Just a Small Change"
**Audience:** tenant / property manager  
**Hook:** "Nothing major changed. Just the usual renewal."  
**Pain:** lease renewal contains an unmentioned notice-period change.  
**Workflow:** Add as New Version → Compare → jump to source → Review → Sign correct version.  
**Hero proof:** 30 days → 90 days.  
**CTA:** Before you renew, know what changed.

## 02. "Updated Offer"
**Audience:** job candidate / HR  
**Hook:** "We cleaned up the wording. Please sign the updated offer."  
**Pain:** salary, start date, probation, or remote-work terms changed.  
**Workflow:** Original/Revised Compare → source evidence → Review → Signed version.  
**Hero proof:** salary or start-date change.  
**CTA:** Before you accept, compare the version you were offered.

## 03. "One More Revision"
**Audience:** freelancer / consultant / agency  
**Hook:** "Final final v7 — no material changes."  
**Pain:** scope, payment term, ownership, or delivery date changes across revisions.  
**Workflow:** Add as New Version → Compare → Review → Sign → Versions.  
**Hero proof:** Net 30 → Net 60.  
**CTA:** Know which version you actually agreed to.

## 04. "The Supplier Amendment"
**Audience:** procurement / operations  
**Hook:** "Same contract, updated appendix."  
**Pain:** price, MOQ, delivery, or termination term changes.  
**Workflow:** Version lineage → Compare → Review comments → approved version.  
**Hero proof:** price per unit or delivery window change.  
**CTA:** Review the change, not the whole document again.

## 05. "The Policy Update"
**Audience:** compliance / admin / HR  
**Hook:** "Please acknowledge the new policy."  
**Pain:** users cannot tell what changed from last policy version.  
**Workflow:** Compare → findings → Review → Activity.  
**Hero proof:** one newly added obligation.  
**CTA:** See the update before you acknowledge it.

# Story Series B — Buried in the Document

## 06. "Page 17"
**Audience:** small business / contract owner  
**Hook:** "I read the whole thing. I still missed the deadline."  
**Pain:** notice, renewal, expiry, or payment date buried inside a long PDF.  
**Workflow:** OCR / Intelligence → Key Fact → exact evidence jump → Reminder.  
**Hero proof:** a date on a specific page.  
**CTA:** Important dates should not stay buried.

## 07. "The Quiet Price Change"
**Audience:** finance / procurement  
**Hook:** approved quote says one amount; later document says another.  
**Pain:** monetary mismatch hidden among many line items.  
**Workflow:** OCR / Intelligence → amount evidence → Review / reminder / follow-up.  
**Hero proof:** approved amount vs invoiced amount.  
**CTA:** Find the number that changed before you pay.

## 08. "Renewal Without a Reminder"
**Audience:** subscription / insurance / lease users  
**Hook:** "It renewed yesterday."  
**Pain:** expiry and renewal dates are known but forgotten.  
**Workflow:** document intelligence → renewal date → Reminder → Workspace.  
**Hero proof:** renewal date extracted with source page.  
**CTA:** Turn a date in a PDF into something you will actually remember.

## 09. "The Account Number"
**Audience:** finance / consumer  
**Hook:** invoice looks identical except for payment details.  
**Pain:** fraudulent or accidental bank-detail changes are easy to miss visually.  
**Workflow:** Compare related invoice versions → Review → hold payment.  
**Hero proof:** account / identifier changed.  
**CTA:** Check the details that matter, not just the total.

# Story Series C — The Paper Trail

## 10. "Which One Did I Sign?"
**Audience:** anyone dealing with revisions  
**Hook:** Contract_final_FINAL2.pdf. Contract_final_signed.pdf. Contract_latest.pdf.  
**Pain:** no one remembers which version became authoritative.  
**Workflow:** Versions → Review history → Signed state → Fingerprint → Activity.  
**Hero proof:** clear lineage from original to reviewed to signed.  
**CTA:** Your document should remember what happened to it.

## 11. "Three Months Later"
**Audience:** SME / freelancer / operations  
**Hook:** "Why did we approve this version?"  
**Pain:** context disappears after email threads go cold.  
**Workflow:** Activity + versions + review comments + fingerprint.  
**Hero proof:** timeline of actions rather than filenames.  
**CTA:** Keep the story behind the file.

## 12. "The File Changed"
**Audience:** privacy / compliance / business  
**Hook:** a saved PDF no longer matches the recorded fingerprint.  
**Pain:** uncertainty about whether bytes changed after review/signing.  
**Workflow:** Fingerprint check → Modified state → inspect current version.  
**Hero proof:** Match vs Modified.  
**CTA:** Know when the file you kept is no longer the file you checked.

## 13. "Send This, Not That"
**Audience:** business / HR / admin  
**Hook:** user almost shares a version containing personal information.  
**Pain:** sensitive data survives in the wrong copy.  
**Workflow:** Review → permanent Redaction → new redacted version → Share.  
**Hero proof:** original preserved, redacted version created.  
**CTA:** Share the safe version without losing the original.

# Story Series D — Restore It

## 14. "The Shadow Across the Page"
**Audience:** consumer / office  
**Hook:** phone photo of a document under bad lighting.  
**Pain:** text is readable to the eye but ugly and poor for OCR.  
**Workflow:** Scan / import → Smart Clean / Remove Shadow → OCR → Search.  
**Hero proof:** before/after real scan.  
**CTA:** Restore documents, not just scan them.

## 15. "The Bent Receipt"
**Audience:** expense / warranty / small business  
**Hook:** crumpled receipt photographed quickly.  
**Pain:** perspective, blur, and poor contrast make it unusable later.  
**Workflow:** dewarp / restore → OCR → folder / search.  
**Hero proof:** search finds an item or merchant after restoration.  
**CTA:** Make paper useful after the moment has passed.

## 16. "The Old Letter"
**Audience:** family / records  
**Hook:** faded page kept in a drawer for years.  
**Pain:** difficult to read, search, or preserve.  
**Workflow:** scan → restore → OCR → archive.  
**Hero proof:** readable cleaned version plus preserved original.  
**CTA:** Keep the original. Make a usable version.

# Story Series E — Life Admin

## 17. "The Warranty Drawer"
**Audience:** homeowner / family  
**Hook:** appliance breaks; nobody can find the receipt.  
**Pain:** receipts, manuals, and warranty dates live in different places.  
**Workflow:** Scan receipt → OCR → folder → reminder → search later.  
**Hero proof:** find receipt by merchant/product and see warranty date.  
**CTA:** Important paperwork should still be useful when you finally need it.

## 18. "Application Day"
**Audience:** visa / school / administrative applicant  
**Hook:** "At least one supporting document is missing."  
**Pain:** many PDFs, scans, expiry dates, and versions.  
**Workflow:** Workspace / folder → OCR/search → review → redact → reminder.  
**Hero proof:** organized set and outstanding action.  
**CTA:** Keep the paperwork together and the next action visible.  
**Limit:** do not claim DocMate guarantees official application completeness.

## 19. "Insurance Claim Folder"
**Audience:** consumer / homeowner  
**Hook:** one incident creates photos, invoices, forms, and correspondence.  
**Pain:** evidence becomes fragmented.  
**Workflow:** import / scan → folder / Workspace → OCR → redact → share.  
**Hero proof:** all claim documents findable and reviewable.  
**CTA:** One incident. One document trail.

## 20. "School Form at 7:42 AM"
**Audience:** parents  
**Hook:** permission form due today, signature needed now.  
**Pain:** paper form arrives late, needs scan/sign/send fast.  
**Workflow:** Scan → clean → Sign → Share → retain copy.  
**Hero proof:** completed document in minutes, signed copy retained.  
**CTA:** From paper on the table to finished document.

# Priority order

## Acquisition stories
1. Lease Renewal
2. Revised Job Offer
3. Freelancer SOW
4. Restore bad scan

These are instantly understandable in under five seconds.

## Monetization stories
1. Procurement / supplier amendment
2. Finance amount mismatch
3. Property management
4. Consultant / SOW revisions
5. Compliance / policy revision

The cost of a mistake is visibly greater than the cost of the app.

## Retention stories
1. Warranty / receipts
2. Renewal reminders
3. Versions / Activity
4. Search / iCloud
5. Fingerprint / document history

These make DocMate worth keeping after the first dramatic use case.

# Standard 30-second theatre structure

**0–3s — Human hook**  
One message, one sentence, one reason to care.

**3–7s — Decision pressure**  
The person is about to sign, pay, send, or ignore.

**7–18s — Real DocMate proof**  
Authentic app UI. One clear finding. Show the source.

**18–24s — Action**  
Review, redact, sign, remind, or choose the correct version.

**24–28s — Durable record**  
Version / Activity / Fingerprint / Workspace.

**28–30s — Brand line**  
One simple promise, not a feature list.

Possible recurring lines:
- **One line can change everything.**
- **Before you sign, know what changed.**
- **Your document should remember what happened to it.**
- **Important dates should not stay buried.**
- **Restore documents, not just scan them.**
